# STAK policy and privacy publication checklist

The policy builder intentionally starts with an unpublished draft. Do not publish it until the following have been confirmed by the responsible STAK decision-makers and, where appropriate, qualified legal/privacy counsel.

- The legal identity and contact details of the entity/person responsible for the processing are correct.
- Each processing purpose is specific and tied to an appropriate legal basis; do not treat the checkbox as blanket GDPR consent.
- The categories of personal data are complete and accurately described (including Discord identifiers, application responses, review notes and any later staff records).
- Discord, Gnomio/Moodle, hosting, database and other processors/recipients are accurately described where applicable.
- Retention periods are defined separately for unsuccessful applicants, successful staff records, moderation records and audit/security logs.
- Data-subject rights, the appropriate supervisory authority/complaint route and contact path are described accurately.
- International transfers and relevant safeguards are addressed if applicable.
- Access is restricted to people who need the information for recruitment or staff-management duties.
- Applicants are not asked to waive non-waivable rights or provide blanket consent to unrestricted disclosure/legal action.
- Any reporting to Discord or a competent authority is framed as a lawful, case-specific measure—not an automatic or unlimited permission.

The publish endpoint checks for unfinished bracket placeholders and draft language. It cannot determine whether a legal basis, retention period or notice is legally correct.
